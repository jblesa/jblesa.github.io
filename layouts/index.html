About

I am a software engineer and engineering manager with more than 15 years of experience developing embedded and real-time systems. My work has focused on UAV autopilots, sensor networks, hardware integration and dependable software products.

I currently serve as Director & Head of Software and IT at UAV Navigation (Grupo Oesía), where I lead the teams responsible for flight-control software, ground-control systems and corporate IT.

Embedded & real-time software

UAV systems

Software architecture & quality
Requirements, verification, CI, release processes and long-lived products.
Engineering leadership
Technical planning, team management and coordination across disciplines.
